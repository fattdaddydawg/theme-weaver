# Theme Weaver — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 11, 2026**

Theme Weaver is a word puzzle game available through Reddit. This Privacy Policy explains how information is used when you play the game.

## 1. Information We Use

Theme Weaver uses your Reddit account identity to associate game progress and results with your account.

Game information may include:

- Your Reddit account identifier and username.
- Puzzle answers, guesses, and hints used.
- Puzzle progress and completion status.
- Scores and completed puzzle results.

We do not require you to provide your name, email address, or payment information to play.

## 2. How Information Is Used

We use this information to:

- Save and restore puzzle progress.
- Calculate scores and maintain permanent results.
- Prevent repeated scored attempts on the same puzzle.
- Display completed games and personal results.
- Maintain game functionality and security.

If competitive leaderboards are introduced, this policy will be updated as appropriate before their launch.

## 3. Data Storage

Theme Weaver is hosted through Reddit's Devvit platform. Game progress and results are stored using Reddit-provided infrastructure.

Theme Weaver does not currently use an independent advertising network, external analytics service, or third-party game database.

Reddit's own collection and processing of information is governed by Reddit's Privacy Policy.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell player information.

Information may be processed through Reddit's infrastructure as necessary to operate the game. Your Reddit username and public interactions may be visible according to Reddit's features and settings.

## 5. Data Retention and Requests

Game progress and finalized scores are retained to provide persistent gameplay and prevent scored replays.

For questions about your game information or requests concerning your data, contact us at **themeweavergame@gmail.com**. We will review requests subject to applicable legal requirements and technical limitations.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Theme Weaver is intended for users who are eligible to use Reddit under Reddit's applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when game features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
